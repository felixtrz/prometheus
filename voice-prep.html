Generates every guide line with @drawcall/generate. Each batch below sends its lines to Drawcall: the page moves to Drawcall, you sign in, the clips generate, and you come back here. The game then loads the same URLs (they are content hashes of text, voice and style, kept in src/game/voice-urls.ts by node scripts/voice-urls.mjs), so no files are copied into the project. Subtitle-only hints are never sent for speech.
