# Game vision

## Hands on crafting

The central fantasy is physically transforming materials into tools, food, and increasingly advanced devices using your hands and VR controllers. Players hold ingredients and components and perform the actions that make an object. An automatically shooting crossbow is an example of the intended technological progression; its exact form and operation remain open.

## Tone and progression

The beginning feels peaceful, with neutral animals populating the world. Enemies become more dangerous as the player progresses. Nighttime primarily changes the atmosphere and adds little extra danger. The condition that advances danger has not yet been chosen.

## Signature moments

Cook a meat stew with your hands in VR. Build a torch and then light the torch you have just made. These moments establish the desired connection between gathering materials, performing physical actions, and using the result.

## Mode and session structure

Single-player only. A satisfying session should last 15–30 minutes. Working assumption to confirm: the world persists between sessions so players return to their ongoing progress. The interview answer about persistence was “between sessions,” without further detail.

## Reference

Don’t Starve is the named reference. Which aspects should influence this game—such as progression, survival pressure, world tone, or visual style—remains to be specified.
