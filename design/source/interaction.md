# Crafting cooking and VR interaction

## Reusable physical actions

Recipes remix reusable interactions to produce different objects. Hammering at the tool bench is one established crafting action. Cooking follows the same principle: put ingredients into a pot and stir it. The action system should support multiple recipes using shared interactions.

## Recipe knowledge

Players can find crafting recipes while exploring. Whether recipes may also be known at the start, unlocked through milestones, or discovered experimentally is undecided. Recipes, quantities, action sequences, and failure conditions have not yet been specified.

## Concrete examples

Meat stew: hold ingredients, put them into a pot, and stir. This is the current interaction concept, not a complete recipe. Preparation, heat management, timing, and serving steps remain open.

Torch: make a torch from materials held and manipulated by the player, then light it. The exact materials, assembly actions, and ignition procedure remain open.

Advanced crossbow: progress toward an automatically shooting crossbow. Whether it is handheld, a mounted defense, or a triggered trap has not been decided.

## Movement

Players move through the world using smooth joystick movement. Turning controls, seated play, reach assistance, and other comfort settings remain to be designed.

## Inventory

Players carry items in a physical backpack that they take off and reach into. Capacity, organization, stacking, backpack placement, and quick access to tools remain open.

## Placement assistance

Objects snap into valid positions when brought close. This supports precise interactions such as placing ingredients or components at a workbench. Snap distance, visual feedback, valid targets, and how players release or remove snapped objects remain open.

## Input scope

Hands and controllers describe the intended physical experience. Controller-free hand tracking has not been confirmed. Supported headsets and input hardware are still to be chosen.
